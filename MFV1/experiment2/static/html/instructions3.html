Instructions (2/3)

Between forecasting tasks, you will be asked to complete a series of word-judgment tasks. You should try to answer as many questions correctly as possible. Your score will not depend on these word-judgment tasks. Do not worry if you do not get them all correct, but please try your best. Monetary compensation is conditional upon sufficient performance on these word-judgment tasks.

A word-judgment task will be a simple yes or no question. Given a word, you will answer one of two questions:

1. Is this item living or not living?

2. Will this item fit in a shoebox?

Once again, your score will only depend on your performance on the forecasting tasks, but please try your best on the word-judgment tasks to ensure monetary compensation.
