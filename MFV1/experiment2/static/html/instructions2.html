Instructions (1/3)

In this experiment, you will be asked to forecast values of a mathematical process.

The process generates a value between 1 and 1000, one at a time. This number depends on previous values in the process as well as random fluctuations. The precise relationship is unknown to you.

Your task is to forecast the next number in the sequence given what you remember as accurately as possible. Once you make a forecast, how accurate your forecast is will be revealed to you on a bar graphic. Use this feedback to improve your future forecasts.

The closer your forecast is to the actual value generated by the process, the more points you will earn. Your monetary compensation will depend on how high your score is at the end of the experiment.
